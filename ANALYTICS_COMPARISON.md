## 📋 Decision Guide

**After reviewing this list:**

1. **Check the boxes** for capabilities you want
2. **Count how many PostHog items** you checked (UX & Design section)
3. **Decide:**
   - If you checked 0-2 PostHog items → Consider not enabling PostHog
   - If you checked 3-5 PostHog items → Enable PostHog with 25% sampling (free tier)
   - If you checked 6+ PostHog items → Enable PostHog with higher sampling (~$67/month)
